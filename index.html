How the project works

From idea to launch

A clear path. No mystery phase.

You always know what I’m working on, what I need from you, and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Understand the business

    We clarify what you offer, who needs it, and what the website should accomplish.

  2. 02

    Choose the right features

    The pages, integrations, and agreed scope are written down before the build.

  3. 03

    Gather your content

    You provide the logo, photos, service details, hours, links, and account invitations we need.

  4. 04

    Design and build

    I create the site around the business instead of forcing it into a recycled template.

  5. 05

    Connect the services

    Selected forms, chat, booking, reviews, maps, videos, email, and analytics are configured.

  6. 06

    Review together

    You see the complete website and use the revisions included in the agreement.

  7. 07

    Test and launch

    Links, displays, integrations, and important visitor paths are checked before launch.

  8. 08

    Hand over with confidence

    You receive basic instructions for the services connected to your website.

Ownership & third parties

Your website should not come with hidden surprises.

The proposal separates my setup work from the outside services that power certain features.

01

You own the accounts. Outside services use your name, email, and billing information.

02

You approve paid upgrades. No subscription is added without your approval.

03

You pay providers directly. My work covers the agreed setup and integration—not third-party charges.

04

Responsibilities stay visible. Chats, forms, bookings, newsletters, and account details remain yours to manage.

Outside companies control their own features, prices, limits, and availability. Current costs and free-plan limits are confirmed before an integration is installed.
